# Jobs and Materials widgets — iPhone setup

This uses the free Scriptable app. You only need your iPhone. The job list and the materials list are snapshots; they do not update by themselves. Ask Jarvis when you want them refreshed.

## Jobs widget

Shows recent job titles.

1. On your iPhone, open the App Store and install **Scriptable** (it's free).
2. Open Safari and go to this page (it is the script):
   https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ericvaknin/text/main/widget/companycam-jobs.js
3. Tap the text, choose **Select All**, then **Copy**.
4. Open Scriptable and tap the **+** button to create a new script.
5. Tap in the empty script, then **Paste**.
6. Tap the name at the top (it may say "Untitled Script") and name it **Jobs**.
7. Tap **Done** (or close the script) to save it.
8. On your Home Screen, press and hold an empty area until the icons jiggle.
9. Tap the **+** in the top corner, search for **Scriptable**, and add a Scriptable widget (Small, Medium, or Large).
   - Small shows 4 jobs. Tapping the widget opens the first job.
   - Medium shows 4 jobs, and you can tap a job to open it in CompanyCam.
   - Large shows up to 10 jobs, also tappable.
10. While the icons are still jiggling, tap the new widget, or press and hold it and tap **Edit Widget**.
11. Under **Script**, choose **Jobs**. Leave everything else as it is.
12. Tap outside the widget, then tap **Done**.

To see the full list, open Scriptable and tap the **Jobs** script. Tap a job in the list to open it in CompanyCam.

## Materials widget

Shows jobs that have an open Materials List in CompanyCam (items still in Unsorted or Order, not yet in Have). The number on the right is how many items are still open.

1. Open Safari and go to this page:
   https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ericvaknin/text/main/widget/companycam-materials.js
2. Tap the text, choose **Select All**, then **Copy**.
3. Open Scriptable and tap the **+** button to create a new script (do not overwrite your Jobs script).
4. Paste the text, name the script **Materials**, and save it.
5. On your Home Screen, press and hold an empty area, tap **+**, and add another Scriptable widget.
   - Small shows 4 jobs. Tapping the widget opens the first job.
   - Medium shows 4 jobs, and you can tap a job to open it in CompanyCam.
   - Large shows up to 10 jobs, also tappable.
6. Edit the widget and set **Script** to **Materials**, then tap **Done**.

To see the full list, open Scriptable and tap the **Materials** script. Tap a job to open it in CompanyCam.
